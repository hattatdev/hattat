# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect and make participation welcoming regardless of background,
identity, disability, or experience. Discuss the work with specific evidence and useful feedback.
Credit original work and respect privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, and publishing private information without consent
are unacceptable. Stop behavior when a maintainer asks you to stop.

Report concerns privately to repository owner `beydemirfurkan` through an available GitHub
contact channel. Do not post private incident details in public issues. If no private contact is
available, ask a maintainer for one without describing the incident publicly.

Maintainers will consider reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation.
Maintainers are subject to the same expectations and should explain moderation decisions
to the affected people when privacy and safety permit.
